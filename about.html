Our Story

Cavendish Hostel Finder was created to solve a real problem faced by students at Cavendish University Uganda finding safe, affordable accommodation around Nsambya, Kabalagala, Kibuli and Kansanga.

As a student at Cavendish University, our founder Olum Walter experienced the difficulty of searching for hostels near campus. This platform was built to make that process simple, transparent, and stress-free for every student.

Today, Cavendish Hostel Finder connects students with verified hostels across Nsambya, Kabalagala, Kibuli and Kansanga, offering a range of options from budget-friendly dormitories to premium private rooms.

Our Mission

To simplify the student housing search process around Cavendish University by providing a trusted platform where students can easily find, compare, and book verified hostel accommodations in Nsambya, Kabalagala, Kibuli and Kansanga that match their budget and preferences.

Our Vision

To become the leading student housing platform for Ugandan universities, creating a world where every student can find quality, affordable accommodation near their campus within minutes, not weeks.
